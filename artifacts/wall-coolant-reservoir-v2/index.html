02 / DESIGN LOGIC

A tank that goes above the pump.

Most of the design is about preserving a favorite silhouette while eliminating needless joints: one ABS reservoir with its outlet already turned through 90°, a removable wide-mouth refill funnel, and a single combined honeycomb cage that mounts on the wall.

A removable funnel guides water into the vented tank from above.

03 / ENGINEERING EVIDENCE

Digitally checked.
Not leak-proof by assumption.

The CAD files are a manufacturing starting point—not a verified working water system. A mesh can be mathematically watertight while a real FDM print leaks through layers, joints, or a stressed hose barb.

Completed in CAD

  • Five printable ABS mesh parts checked for manifold/watertight solid geometry
  • Nominal assembly interference-volume checks performed
  • Three plate layouts checked to fit the Bambu P2S build volume
  • 3/8″-ID outlet/return concept and wall mount documented

Still needs physical validation

  • Actual print integrity and support removal at the elbow
  • ABS layer bond, hose grip, clamps and strain relief
  • Gasket compression around clear sight plate and lid
  • 24-hour cold-water leak test, warm-water cycles, circulating pump test and mounting-load check

What's not ABS?

The clear sight plate is planned as 86 × 110 × 3 mm polycarbonate sheet, with compliant silicone gaskets and actual screws/inserts. Those materials are needed to retain the reference design and seal the reservoir.

Operating boundary

Use only as an unpressurized, vented suction-side reservoir. The previously discussed 190°F was a question, not a target. No continuous safe operating temperature, leak resistance, or pumped performance has been established.
